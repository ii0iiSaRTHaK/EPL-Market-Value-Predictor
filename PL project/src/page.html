Find the player

Names, positions, and season statistics come from public Fantasy Premier League datasets. Search across five season snapshots, including historical players.

02

Read the performance

Goals, assists, minutes, and cards from the selected and preceding seasons become inputs to the saved XGBoost model.

03

Keep the context

This is an experimental market-value estimate, not a transfer fee. Age, height, appearances, and several profile fields are unavailable. Full-season totals differ from the model’s rolling-year training inputs.

What does the evaluation tell us?+

The saved model’s historical holdout median absolute percentage error was 47.9%, with RMSE around €14.4 million. Those results describe the historical test set; they do not measure the incomplete input setup used in this app.
